# MedSpa — Luxury Medical Spa & Cosmetic Clinic HTML5 Template

MedSpa is a modern, standalone, zero-dependency HTML5/CSS3/Vanilla JS website template crafted for aesthetic medicine clinics, cosmetic surgeons, dermatology centers, medical spas, and wellness sanctuaries.

Inspired by top-tier medical aesthetic experiences, it features clean semantic architecture, mobile-first responsive layouts, interactive procedure hotspots, draggable before & after transformation sliders, a slide-out shopping bag, a live treatment search modal, and an interactive appointment scheduling system.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Flagship Homepage (`index.html`)**:
   - **Hero Slider**: Full-width interactive hero slider featuring smooth slide transitions, custom typography (`Fraunces` & `Figtree`), bullet pagination, and keyboard/touch navigation.
   - **4 Core Clinical Pillars**: Highlighting Advanced Expertise, Cutting-Edge FDA-Cleared Technology, Natural Results, and Tailored Personalized Care.
   - **Philosophy & Brand Story**: 4-photo staggered editorial montage with an animated rotating circular stamp badge (*14+ Years of Aesthetic Excellence*).
   - **Interactive Body Silhouette Hotspots**: Pulse-animated markers placed across key anatomical zones (Face, Decollete, Waist, Thighs) that trigger dynamic floating cards with procedure specs, duration, and pricing.
   - **Draggable Before & After Comparison Slider**: Superimposed dual-image viewport with a touch/mouse draggable center handle revealing clinical results before and after treatment across 5 distinct clinical cases.
   - **Signature Treatments Directory**: Filterable procedures grid (`All`, `Facial`, `Body`, `Laser`, `Injectables`, `Hair`) with duration, pricing, and 1-click booking modal triggers.
   - **Clinical Essentials Skincare Shop**: Product cards with discount badges, star ratings, Quick View modal, Wishlist toggle, and Add to Bag functionality.
   - **Patient Reviews Carousel**: Verified patient testimonials with star ratings, treatment procedure badges, and avatar quotes.
   - **Trusted Brand Partners**: Infinite marquee loop of medical aesthetic industry partners.
   - **Clinic Visit & Appointment Booking**: Interior sanctuary showcase with direct concierge phone, location map details, direct WhatsApp consultation trigger, and an interactive booking inquiry form.
   - **FAQ Accordion**: Smooth collapsible FAQ accordion for common patient questions.
   - **Beauty Journal**: Clinical insight cards with categories, read times, and publication dates.
   - **Luxury Multi-Column Footer**: Newsletter subscription box, operating hours, quick links, and WhatsApp quick chat.

2. **Dedicated Subpages**:
   - **`about-us.html`**: Clinic philosophy, four clinical standards, accreditation, and patient volume metrics.
   - **`treatments.html`**: Full procedure catalog with specialty category filtering, downtime indicators, and pricing.
   - **`before-after.html`**: Dedicated clinical transformation gallery with draggable comparison sliders and case studies.
   - **`doctors.html`**: Faculty profiles for board-certified plastic surgeons, dermatologists, master injectors, and licensed aestheticians.
   - **`pricing.html`**: Transparent treatment pricing tiers, package discounts, and 3-tier VIP Sanctuary Care Memberships (*Radiance Glow*, *Clinical Silhouette*, *Couture Bespoke*).
   - **`shop.html`**: Clinical homecare skincare shop with category filters, quick view, wishlist, and bag drawer.
   - **`blog.html`**: Editorial beauty journal featuring dermatological insights and treatment guides.
   - **`contact.html`**: Beverly Hills sanctuary location, operating schedule, direct phone/email, and inquiry form.

3. **Interactive UI Systems & Modals**:
   - **Slide-out Cart Drawer**: Live subtotal calculations, item quantity adjustment, item removal, and checkout simulation.
   - **Global Book Appointment Modal**: Multi-step scheduler with procedure prefill, doctor selection, date & time slot selection, and `localStorage` booking persistence.
   - **Live Search Modal**: Real-time search across all treatments and skincare products with instant filtered results.
   - **Toast Notification Engine**: Floating status alerts for cart additions, wishlist updates, newsletter signups, and booking confirmations.
   - **Back to Top Button**: Smooth scroll to top with dynamic scroll-distance visibility.

4. **Zero WordPress Footprint**:
   - 100% original semantic HTML5 markup.
   - Custom `.ae-` CSS namespace.
   - Pure Vanilla JS (`assets/js/app.js` and `assets/js/data.js`).
   - Clean local image naming and custom SVG logos (`logo.svg`, `logo-white.svg`, `favicon.svg`).
